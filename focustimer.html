Focus Timer – Privacy Policy

App: Focus Timer · Effective date: November 7, 2026

Summary

Focus Timer works entirely offline on your watch. It does not collect, upload, sell or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Information stored on your device

The app does not save any data on your watch. Your timer settings and today's session count exist only while the app is open.

Permissions

  • ohos.permission.VIBRATE: Vibrates when a focus, rest or workout interval ends.

The app does not request internet, location, contacts, camera, microphone or health sensor access.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Focus Timer 隐私政策

应用：Focus Timer · 生效日期：2026年11月7日

概述

Focus Timer 完全在您的手表上离线运行，不收集、上传、出售或共享任何个人数据。无需账号，无广告，无数据分析。

保存在设备上的信息

应用不会在手表上保存任何数据。计时设置和当天的专注次数仅在应用打开期间存在。

权限

  • ohos.permission.VIBRATE：专注、休息或训练时段结束时振动提醒。

应用不申请网络、位置、通讯录、相机、麦克风或健康传感器权限。

儿童

应用不会有意收集任何人的信息，包括 13 岁以下儿童。

政策变更

如本政策有更新，新版本将发布在本页面并注明新的生效日期。
